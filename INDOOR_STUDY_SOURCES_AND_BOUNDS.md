# Physical bounds and evidence
The parent study's geometry/material constants and reciprocal imposed-drag surrogate are retained. This experiment changes actual passive hinge geometry, not arbitrary tensor coefficients or allowed strain.

Primary research: Malka, Lussier Desbiens, Chen and Wood, Principles of Microscale Flexure Hinge Design for Enhanced Endurance (IROS2014), https://smrl.mit.edu/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/iros14_malka.pdf . Their experimentally studied polyimide/carbon-fiber laminated bending hinges show fatigue initiation near corners and emphasize coupled stiffness/stress design. Their films are7.5/12.7µm thick, far thicker than our assumed120/240nm ribbons. Their bending stiffness formula must NOT be substituted for our torsional-strip formula. This source motivates geometry/fatigue checks, not numerical validation of our model or manufacturing process.

The retained torsion surrogate uses G=E/[2(1+nu)], J≈b h³(1−.63h/b)/3, k=GJ/L, strain≈h beta/L, and hinge mass=rho L b h. E2.5GPa,nu.4,rho1400kg/m³,1%shearstrain and80/120/240nm thin-film behavior remain assumptions. Length/thickness variations preserve small local strain but finite global rotation. This does not resolve clamp-induced warping, stress concentration, bonding, surface-size effects or fatigue. A linear moment-angle law is not enough to qualify20-minute life.

Damping.02 and.10 are positive-dissipation sensitivity cases. Changing damping is NOT a free actuator control or proven material option. It requires measured dynamic torque-angle hysteresis, or a specified added damping element with its geometry/mass/energy effects. No such mechanism is yet qualified, so damping-sensitive success must be reported separately from geometry-only success.

Missing decisive measurements: loaded strip torque-angle loops at237–267Hz and operating amplitude; cyclic failure including clamps/bonds; wing mass distribution/pitch axis; aerodynamic pitching moment versus stroke and pitch. The underlying normal-drag resultant remains uncalibrated and omits full low-Re unsteady wake, bristle interaction, added mass and complete geometric inertia. All force/flight/endurance conclusions remain excluded.
